# tectible.co

**I help companies build AI that actually reaches production.**

## About

[tectible.co](https://tectible.co) is the professional website of **Pablo Baretto**.

With over 20 years of experience architecting and building large-scale data systems for companies such as **Orange Telecom** and **Telefónica**, Pablo has spent the last several years designing and shipping production-grade AI applications for mid-market teams.

He now focuses on helping mid-market companies overcome the most common AI pitfalls and turn promising prototypes into reliable, scalable systems that deliver real business value.

## Common Challenges Pablo Solves

- CTOs struggling to define where AI starts, how it fits their company, and what the real ROI is
- Promising prototypes that look great in demos but fall apart (or become unreliable) once they hit real production
- AI outputs drifting, hallucinating, or showing biases at the worst possible moments
- Strong engineering teams that still aren’t using AI tools and practices to work 10× faster
