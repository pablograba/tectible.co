# Pablo G. Baretto here.

Most mid-market companies don’t have an AI problem. They have a process, data, and ownership problem that AI vendors are happy to wrap in a chatbot.

I work with companies of 50 to 300 people. They already have at least ERP and CRM, a small IT team, and a leadership group under pressure to “do something with AI.” I help them decide what is worth building, what to buy, and what to ignore, so the company gets value from AI.

Typical work takes 4 to 16 weeks:

- I start from the company’s constraints and design an AI stack around them: in-house capacity and skills, privacy requirements, preferred cost structure, existing IT-vendor agreements, and cloud or on-prem footprint.
- Then I implement the stack, starting with a few common and low-risk use cases. That is usually an internal chatbot and an accurate knowledge base (RAG) application. After that, if they want, a stack the internal team can run. I don’t stay forever and I don’t sell software.

Recently I implemented an AI stack for a biotechnology R&D firm. It eliminated shadow AI and cut research time by 50% average, with an internal scientific knowledge base they can query in natural language.

Over the last 20+ years I’ve been doing IT solutions architecture, data engineering, and in recent years AI software engineering.
